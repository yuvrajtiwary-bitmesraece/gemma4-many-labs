## P1. Consensus on task difficulty

- Tasks attempted by at least 5 labs: **101** of 129.
- Split-half reliability of consensus difficulty (Spearman, 2000 random splits of the labs): median **0.80** (2.5-97.5 percentile 0.61 to 0.88), Spearman-Brown corrected **0.89**; median 103 tasks per split.
- Pairwise agreement between labs sharing at least 30 tasks: 39 pairs, median Spearman 0.65 (interquartile range 0.51 to 0.82, minimum -0.09, maximum 0.93).
- Floor (consensus solve rate at most 10%): **40** tasks, of which 25 were never resolved by any lab. Ceiling (at least 90%): **3**. Discriminating (in between): **58**.
- Consensus solve rate: mean 26.6%, median 12.5%, quartiles 0.2% and 50.0%.

## P3. Replicate noise and detectable differences

- 123 pairs of repeated runs, 49 systems, 9 labs; median 26 shared tasks per pair.
- **Flip rate** (share of shared tasks whose outcome differs between two repeats): pooled **11.2%**; across labs median 10.4%, interquartile range 10.0% to 13.4%, range 0.0% to 16.7%.
- Absolute difference in resolve rate between two repeats: pooled 3.2 points; across labs median 5.8 points, range 0.0 to 16.7 points; largest single pair 25.0 points; 13.8% of pairs had identical outcomes on every task.
- Minimum detectable improvement for one paired comparison (exact two-sided McNemar test, α = 0.05, power 0.80, discordance = flip rate + improvement): **34.8 points** on 30 tasks, **20.8 points** on 60 tasks, **12.5 points** on 129 tasks.
- Consistency check fixed in advance: pooled flip rate 11.2% against twice the replicate-noise variance 10.8% (unweighted).

## P4. Subset comparability

- Rasch model on 260 systems and 129 tasks (converged: True). Reference system: median ability among the 193 systems labelled 31B.
- Runs used **52** distinct task subsets of at least 10 tasks. Predicted resolve rate of the reference system: **5.7% to 66.7%** (a spread of 60.9 points; interquartile range 30.2% to 38.5%); on the full task set 32.9%.
- Restricted to the 22 subsets of at least 30 tasks: 25.5% to 57.8%.
- Model check (not fixed in advance): predicted against observed resolve rate per run, Pearson 0.99, mean absolute error 1.4 points; Brier score 0.065 against 0.189 for the base rate.
